# The Code and the Dream

In the quiet hours of night,
When the screen casts its gentle light,
A coder sits with thoughtful gaze,
Building worlds in digital haze.

Lines of logic, clean and clear,
Transforming thought to something near
To magic—functions, loops, and calls,
Creating beauty within walls.

Each semicolon, each bracket placed,
A careful step, no time to waste,
For in this realm of ones and zeros,
We craft our dreams like modern heroes.

The compiler hums, the tests run green,
A symphony of code unseen,
And in that moment, pure and bright,
The abstract becomes concrete light.

So here's to those who build and dream,
Who turn the impossible to seem
As simple as a morning breeze,
In lines of code that never cease.
